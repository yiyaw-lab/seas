# Agent Organization Lab Scoring Rubric

Score 1-10

## Originality

Does it produce a surprising idea?

## Frontierness

Does it touch a frontier question?

## Builder Interest

Would frontier builders care?

## Capability Gain

Would building it teach something valuable?

## Artifact Potential

Could it become a repo, benchmark, paper, dataset, or public writeup?

## Total

Average of all five scores.
