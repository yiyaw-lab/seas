Observation 1

“Subagents are a context-management technology.”

Weakness:

This assumes context isolation is the primary source of value.

Hidden assumption:

The bottleneck is context contamination.

Alternative:

The bottleneck may actually be organizational specialization.

The value might come from role differentiation rather than context isolation.

Observation 2

“The frontier is shifting from model-centric optimization to organization-centric optimization.”

Weakness:

This frames the question as a replacement.

Hidden assumption:

Model quality and organizational quality compete.

More important question:

Do they multiply?

A mediocre model in a great organization may outperform a strong model in a poor organization.

The frontier may be interaction effects, not substitution.

⸻

Observation 3

“There is a measurement gap.”

Weakness:

Measurement itself is not the opportunity.

Everybody notices measurement gaps.

The opportunity is creating the benchmark that becomes the standard.

⸻

Observation 4

“Subagents create a new design space.”

Weakness:

This is descriptive.

Not explanatory.

The interesting question is:

Which dimensions of organizational design actually matter?

Most design spaces are huge.

Only a few variables usually matter.
