# SEAS-001: Agent Organization Lab

## Hypothesis

Different organizational structures produce different kinds of thinking.

The quality of AI outputs may depend as much on organizational structure as on model quality.

## Motivation

Most frontier AI work focuses on:

- larger models
- better prompts
- more tools

Much less attention is given to:

- organizational structure
- delegation patterns
- coordination mechanisms
- information flow

SEAS itself may ultimately depend on discovering which structures are best suited for different kinds of thinking.

## Core Question

Which organizational structures produce which thinking modes?

## Thinking Modes

- Benchmark Thinking
- Theory Thinking
- Systems Thinking
- Optimization Thinking
- Exploratory Thinking
- Design Thinking
- Evaluation Thinking
- Mechanistic Thinking
- Strategic Thinking
- First-Principles Thinking
- Opportunity Thinking

## Candidate Structures

### 1. Single Agent

One model receives the task.

### 2. Researcher + Critic

Researcher generates ideas.
Critic attacks assumptions and identifies deeper opportunities.

### 3. Debate Team

Two agents propose competing viewpoints.
Judge synthesizes.

### 4. Specialist Team

Researcher
Builder
Evaluator

### 5. Manager + Specialists

Manager coordinates specialized agents and synthesizes outputs.

## Experimental Task

Given the same frontier signal:

Claude Code Subagents

Generate the highest-leverage opportunity.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Originality
- Capability Gain
- Artifact Potential
- Ecosystem Leverage
- Frontierness
- Builder Interest
- Delight

## Deliverables

1. Benchmark dataset
2. Opportunity outputs from each structure
3. Comparative analysis
4. Organizational structure map
5. Public writeup
6. GitHub repository

## Success Criteria

Produce evidence that:

1. Different structures generate different kinds of thinking.
2. Certain structures outperform others for specific thinking modes.
3. SEAS can intentionally choose organizational structures based on desired outputs.

## Potential Implications

If true:

- SEAS becomes an organizational intelligence system.
- Agent architecture becomes more important than individual prompts.
- Organizational design becomes a frontier capability.
- Similar principles may apply to:
  - AI systems
  - companies
  - research labs
  - human teams
